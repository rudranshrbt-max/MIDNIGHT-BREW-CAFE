Our Story

Crafted with passion,
served with soul

Born from a love of exceptional coffee and the desire to create a space where the world slows down, The Midnight Brew Cafe is Rawatbhata's most treasured haven for coffee enthusiasts and dreamers alike.

We source only the finest single-origin beans, roasted in small batches to unlock their full character. Every cup is a journey — from the hills where the beans were grown to the moment it warms your hands.
